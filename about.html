ABOUT ALPINE ASCENTS

Curiosity, capability and respect for the mountains.

OUR PURPOSE

Make mountain knowledge accessible.

Alpine Ascents is an educational mountaineering portal created to introduce the mountain environment, explain the many forms of climbing and encourage informed participation.

Mountains reward preparation and sound judgement. Our aim is to bring essential topics together in one welcoming place: history, styles, techniques, shelter, hazards, records, organizations and current developments.

This website is informational. It does not replace qualified instruction, local route knowledge, professional guiding or current conditions reports.

OUR COMMITMENT

Adventure with perspective.

The mountains are not a stage to conquer. They are complex natural environments that deserve care. We encourage learning, gradual skill development, responsible travel and the willingness to turn around when conditions or judgement call for it.
