SHELTER IN THE MOUNTAINS

Choose shelter with conditions in mind.

Mountain environments can expose travellers to wind, cold, precipitation and rapidly changing weather. Overnight plans should account for the route, season, forecast, local regulations and the abilities of everyone in the group.

Established huts, designated campsites and suitable tents each serve different settings. Availability, access rules and seasonal operation vary by region, so verify details with local authorities or hut operators before departure.

Tent camps

Portable shelter for permitted areas. Select durable, appropriate equipment and follow local camping rules.

Mountain huts

Managed refuges can provide rest and basic facilities. Check reservations, services and seasonal opening.

Emergency shelter

Understand the emergency options in your region and carry suitable backup protection for your trip.

BEFORE YOU STAY

Overnight planning checklist

  • Confirm whether camping or hut use is permitted.
  • Check forecast, exposure, temperature and expected conditions.
  • Choose shelter appropriate to the environment and group.
  • Plan water, food, waste management and emergency communication.
  • Leave the site clean and avoid disturbing wildlife.
