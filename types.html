MOUNTAINEERING / 02

FIND YOUR
CLIMBING STYLE

Different terrain calls for different skills, experience and preparation.

Mountaineering covers a broad family of activities. The distinctions below are useful starting points, but real routes often combine several disciplines and can change character with weather and conditions.

01 / HIGH MOUNTAIN

Alpine climbing

Routes in mountain environments that may combine rock, snow, ice, glaciers and changing weather.

02 / ROCK

Rock climbing

Climbing natural rock features using movement, route-finding, protection systems and trained partners.

03 / FROZEN TERRAIN

Ice climbing

Progression on frozen waterfalls or ice formations, requiring specialist equipment and instruction.

04 / APPROACH

Trekking & mountaineering

Long journeys on foot through mountain landscapes, sometimes involving non-technical high routes.

05 / EXPEDITION

High-altitude expeditions

Extended journeys to high peaks that require careful acclimatization, logistics and experienced teams.

06 / WINTER

Winter mountaineering

Mountain travel in cold-season conditions, where snowpack, avalanche awareness and weather are central.
