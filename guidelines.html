GENERAL GUIDELINES

Respect your limits and the environment.

These general principles are intended as an educational starting point. Requirements differ by country, route, season and activity. Always follow local regulations and seek qualified guidance for technical objectives.

01

Plan thoroughly

Research the route, access, expected duration, weather, daylight and available emergency support.

02

Match the objective

Choose a route that fits the group's experience, fitness, equipment and current conditions.

03

Check conditions

Use reliable weather and, where relevant, avalanche and mountain-condition bulletins.

04

Tell someone

Share your itinerary and expected return time with a trusted contact; update them if plans change.

05

Travel as a team

Agree on communication, regrouping points and decision-making before starting.

06

Carry suitable equipment

Know how to use and inspect your equipment. Carry appropriate clothing, navigation and emergency supplies.

07

Protect the environment

Follow Leave No Trace principles, respect wildlife and local communities, and manage waste responsibly.

08

Be ready to turn back

Set decision points in advance. Turning around can be the safest and most successful choice.

Remember: No website can certify readiness for a climb. For technical mountaineering, glacier travel, avalanche terrain or high-altitude objectives, obtain recognized training and use qualified local professionals.
