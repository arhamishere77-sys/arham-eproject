MOUNTAINEERING / 03

SKILL IS BUILT
STEP BY STEP

Good technique begins with instruction, practice and sound decisions.

THE FUNDAMENTALS

Preparation is a technical skill.

Mountaineering is not one technique. It is a combination of physical movement, route planning, weather awareness, communication and risk management. The right methods depend on the terrain and conditions.

Practical skills should be learned in person from qualified instructors and practiced in controlled settings before being relied upon in remote environments.

01

Route planning

Study maps, route descriptions, escape options, timing and access restrictions. Share a realistic itinerary with a trusted contact.

02

Navigation

Develop map, compass and GPS literacy. Understand that electronic devices can fail and visibility can change quickly.

03

Movement skills

Build balance, efficient footwork and terrain awareness under qualified supervision, appropriate to the route.

04

Weather judgement

Check reliable forecasts and understand how wind, temperature, precipitation and visibility affect the plan.

05

Team communication

Agree roles, check-ins, decision points and a shared plan for changing conditions before setting out.

06

Equipment knowledge

Learn the correct use, inspection, fit and limitations of equipment with an experienced instructor.

Important: This page is an introduction, not a technical training manual. Do not attempt rope systems, glacier travel, steep snow or ice techniques based only on website instructions. Seek qualified, hands-on training.
